Brand guidelines · v2.0 Night Watch · October 2026

One raven, used the same way everywhere.

In Norse myth, Odin's raven Huginn, whose name means "thought", flies out each day and returns with what it has learned. Our amber full stop is the raven's eye: the moment a thought lands. This page shows how to use the brand, where to use each file, and what never to do.

Display: one weight, tight

Headlines use one weight, 500, with tight em-based tracking, so size and space carry the emphasis. The split weight (Regular + Bold) belongs to the wordmark; v1 headlines borrowed it, v2 headlines don't.

Tools for teams building with AI agents.

font: var(--tr-display-xl); letter-spacing: var(--tr-display-xl-tracking);

Do

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons and tabs
  • Medium 500 with tight em tracking for display
  • 60–75 characters per line for long text
  • Tabular numbers in tables and prices

Don't

  • Light weights (300), or mixed weights, in headlines
  • Title Case or ALL CAPS headlines (kickers are the only caps)
  • Inter or Roboto as substitutes for display
  • Retyping the wordmark

Motion and icons

Calm, quick, purposeful. Motion shows that the raven is thinking or that something changed, never spectacle.

Fades and small moves only (≤ 8 px). No bounces, springs or fake typewriter text. With reduced motion turned on, the dots stay still at full opacity.

Usage rules

Simple rules keep the raven recognisable everywhere.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the wordmark at least the height of its "t".

Minimum size

Wordmark: 100 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Monogram: 16 px.

Do and don't

Ink on light
Bone on dark
Don't stretch
Don't skew or italicise
Don't rotate
Don't recolour
Don't add glows or shadows
Don't place on busy backgrounds
Thinking Raven
No amber text on light

Accessibility checklist

  • All text tokens meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) on their surfaces
  • Amber is never text on light backgrounds (1.7:1)
  • Visible focus: ink ring on light, amber ring on dark
  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44 px
  • Never colour alone: pair status with an icon or a word
  • Respect reduced motion: static thinking dots
  • Logo alt text is "Thinking Raven"; decorative art uses alt=""
  • Stream AI answers into an aria-live="polite" region

Thinky, the mascot

Thinky gives the product a friendly face. The wordmark carries the company name; Thinky lives inside the app.

Use Thinky for

  • The app icon
  • Onboarding, empty states and loading screens
  • Stickers, merch and community posts

Don't

  • Use Thinky as the company logo
  • Place the black raven on dark backgrounds
  • Change its colours or proportions
  • Redraw it or create new poses without a design review

Voice

Calm, curious, precise. We think first, speak second.

We sound like

  • Short sentences and plain words
  • Specific claims over grand ones
  • Confident, never hyped
  • A little raven imagery, used sparingly ("Something is hatching")
  • Sentence case for headings and buttons

We avoid

  • "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "unleash", "supercharge", "cutting-edge"
  • Exclamation marks in product copy
  • Writing the name as "ThinkingRaven" or "TR" in sentences
