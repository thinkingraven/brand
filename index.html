Brand guidelines · v1.0 · October 2026

One raven, used the same way everywhere.

In Norse myth, Odin's raven Huginn, whose name means "thought", flies out each day and returns with what it has learned. Our amber full stop is the raven's eye: the moment a thought lands. This page shows how to use the brand, where to use each file, and what never to do.

Usage rules

Simple rules keep the raven recognisable everywhere.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the wordmark at least the height of its "t".

Minimum size

Wordmark: 100 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Monogram: 16 px.

Do and don't

Ink on light
Bone on dark
Don't stretch
Don't skew or italicise
Don't rotate
Don't recolour
Don't add glows or shadows
Don't place on busy backgrounds
Thinking Raven
No amber text on light

Thinky, the mascot

Thinky gives the product a friendly face. The wordmark carries the company name; Thinky lives inside the app.

Use Thinky for

  • The app icon
  • Onboarding, empty states and loading screens
  • Stickers, merch and community posts

Don't

  • Use Thinky as the company logo
  • Place the black raven on dark backgrounds
  • Change its colours or proportions
  • Redraw it or create new poses without a design review

Voice

Calm, curious, precise. We think first, speak second.

We sound like

  • Short sentences and plain words
  • Specific claims over grand ones
  • Confident, never hyped
  • A little raven imagery, used sparingly ("Something is hatching")
  • Sentence case for headings and buttons

We avoid

  • "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "unleash", "supercharge", "cutting-edge"
  • Exclamation marks in product copy
  • Writing the name as "ThinkingRaven" or "TR" in sentences
