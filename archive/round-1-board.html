Thinking Raven · Logo exploration · round 1

Nine directions, one raven

Every concept uses the same disciplined palette, so whichever you pick works with the rest of the system. Each card shows the mark on light and dark, as an X/LinkedIn avatar, as a browser favicon at real size, and in an email signature. Tick the ones you like and send them back. The next round refines your favourites into the final logo package (ICO, PNG sizes, social kit).

01

Modern wordmark

Text

Lowercase geometric sans. "thinking" is light and "raven" is bold, and the amber full stop is the raven's eye, the thought landing. Quiet, confident, very usable.

02

Editorial wordmark

Text

A high-contrast serif with an italic "Raven". Feels like a literary imprint or a research institute: timeless, calm and expensive.

03

Speaking raven

Minimalist

One shape with two readings: a raven's head, and a speech bubble whose tail is the beak. That's "thinks before it speaks" in a single mark. Still legible at 16 px.

04

R lettermark

Lettermark

A bold "R" whose bowl is the raven's head: the counter becomes the amber eye, and a beak breaks out of the curve. Reads as a letter first and a bird second.

05

Thinking dots

AI-native

Every AI chat shows "• • •" while it thinks. Here the third dot hatches into the raven. Instantly readable as AI, and it animates naturally in a loading state.

06

Amber block

In your face

A full-bleed amber square with a giant cropped raven staring out of it, plus a heavy stacked wordmark. Built to stop the thumb in an X or LinkedIn feed.

07

Huginn seal

Emblem

A circular seal with the raven inside and "THOUGHT & MEMORY" around it, after Odin's ravens Huginn and Muninn. Heritage feel; great for stickers, merch and the "about" page.

08

Thought & Memory

Abstract

Huginn ("thought") and Muninn ("memory") as two interlocking raven heads, turned 180° like a yin-yang. One eye glows amber, the other is negative space.

09

"Thinky" mascot

Mascot

A friendly raven with a big curious eye and three thought bubbles rising. It gives the brand a face for the app icon, onboarding and stickers, while the serious marks carry the name.
